Our Story

✨ Welcome to Haus of Crumbs

Where every crumb carries comfort, care, and a little bit of home. Founded in 2021, Haus of Crumbs began as a simple love for baking, a way to share warmth and nostalgia through homemade treats crafted with quality and heart.

What started in a small home kitchen has grown into a cherished local brand, known for its dedication to flavour, detail, and that signature homemade touch. Each bake is thoughtfully made, blending timeless recipes with a personal twist that makes every bite feel special. At Haus of Crumbs, it’s more than just dessert, its sweetness made memorable. 🍰
